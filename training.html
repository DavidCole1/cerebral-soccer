The 360 training model

Train every side of your game

Every session connects clean technique to movement, awareness, decisions, and confidence under pressure. We train the skills that matches actually demand.

One on one

Private training

Private training gives one player the time and attention to work on the details that matter most. We start with a real assessment, then build a program around what I see.

During the first session, I watch how you receive, strike, move, and make decisions. From there, each session has a clear purpose and ends with something useful you can practice on your own.

  • Individual development plan after the first session
  • Technical and tactical work for your position
  • Progress updates that parents can understand
  • Training homework between sessions

2 to 4 players

Small group training

Some parts of soccer need another player. Small group sessions add pressure, competition, and the quick choices that make training feel more like a match. Groups stay small, so every player gets personal feedback and every repetition has a purpose.

I can place your player in a group or build one with people they already know. Bring a teammate, sibling, or a few friends from your club, and I will match the level.

  • Groups matched by age and level
  • 1v1, 2v2, and small group decision games
  • Constant individual correction within the group
  • Option to bring your own group

The 360 approach

Every session is built around passing, receiving, and dribbling because those are the skills every player uses most often in a match.

  1. Stations Three stations, one plan

    Each station focuses on a specific need, so the session stays useful and the player understands what we are working toward.

  2. Tactics The bigger picture in every drill

    Every exercise includes a game problem, so players understand why a touch or movement matters.

  3. Scanning Decision making under pressure

    Players practice checking their surroundings before the ball arrives, helping them act sooner and make better choices under pressure.

Questions

Training FAQ

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions take place at fields in Alexandria and across Northern Virginia. I confirm the exact location when we set your schedule and keep travel reasonable for families.

How often should my player train?

Most players make steady progress with one session a week alongside their club schedule. Some players train twice a week when preparing for tryouts, a new level, or college. After the first evaluation, I will recommend what makes sense for your player.

What does it cost?

Rates depend on whether you choose private or small group training and how often you train. Send an inquiry and I will include the current rate sheet in my reply.

Do you work with goalkeepers?

My specialty is field players. Goalkeepers are welcome to work on distribution, footwork, and playing out from the back. For dedicated shot stopping work, I can point you to a specialist.

My player already trains with their club. Why add this?

Club sessions develop the team. Private training gives one player more purposeful touches, personal feedback, and time to work on the details that matter most to their game.
