Questions

Film Room FAQ

How should we film the game?

A wide, steady view is best. Place a phone on a tripod near midfield and film from a few rows up if possible. Follow the ball loosely so I can also see the players around it. Footage from Veo or Trace works well too.

Full match or highlights?

Please send the full match. Highlights show what went well, but the rest of the game shows positioning away from the ball, reactions after mistakes, and runs that did not receive a pass. Those moments are often where the most useful lessons are found.

How long does it take?

Your written breakdown arrives within five days. During busy periods such as tryouts or playoffs, I will share the current timing before you commit.

Who sees my video?

Only I see your video. I do not share or post private links. You can also send an unlisted link that works only for people who have it.

Does this work if I train with another club or coach?

Yes. The Film Room works remotely and is independent of any club. Your team coach handles the group, while this review gives you personal feedback on your own game.
