The 360 method

Build the complete player

Soccer gets easier when you see the picture early. A clean touch matters, but it matters more when it is connected to awareness, movement, quick thinking, and confidence under pressure.

My job is to connect those pieces. Every exercise has a reason, and every repetition includes a decision. When film can help, we use it to show players what was hard to notice in the moment.
