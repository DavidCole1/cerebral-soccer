The 360 method

Build the complete player

A great player is more than one clean touch. Technique has to connect to awareness, movement, speed of thought, and confidence under pressure. That is the full 360° of development — every side of the player, trained as one game.

I build sessions around what matches actually demand, with a decision attached to every repetition. Film then reveals the angles a player cannot feel in the moment. The result is not just more work — it is clearer, more complete development.
