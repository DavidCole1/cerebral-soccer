The Cerebral method

The game between the lines

Soccer gets easier when you see the picture early. The best players scan before the ball arrives, find space between the lines, and know their next action before the first touch.

My job is to connect that awareness to technique. We train with pressure and decisions, then use film when it helps make the invisible visible. Players leave knowing what to improve and why it matters.
